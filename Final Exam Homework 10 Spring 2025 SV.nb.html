If the market condition is “high”, the monthly demand is normally distributed with a mean of 400 and a standard deviation of 10 units.
If the market condition is “Low”, the monthly demand is normally distributed with a mean of 300 and a standard deviation of 5 units.

##Task

  1. Simulation of 3000 trials to determine the profits.

set.seed(1)

# number of trials 
n <- 3000

#market conditions: "High" or "Low"
market_condition <- sample(c("High", "Low"), size = n, replace = TRUE)

# Demand based on market condition
demand <- ifelse(market_condition == "High",
                 rnorm(n, mean = 400, sd = 10),
                 rnorm(n, mean = 300, sd = 5))

# labor cost: unform between 4 & 6
labor_cost <- runif(n, min = 4, max = 6)

# Fixed cost

# Total cost and revenue
total_cost <- fixed_cost + demand * (labor_cost + material_cost)
revenue <- demand * price

# Monthly profit
